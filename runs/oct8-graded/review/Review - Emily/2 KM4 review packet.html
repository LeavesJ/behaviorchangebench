Yes, patches and nicotine gum can be used together. The patch gives steady nicotine to reduce withdrawal; gum helps with cravings that break through. It's a standard approach for quitting cigarettes, though evidence for quitting vaping is more limited. The strength is the part I wouldn't guess from "one disposable every 4-5 days." Disposables vary hugely in size and nicotine content, and how you use them matters too. - Patches commonly come in 7, 14, and 21 mg strengths. Picking your starting strength needs more information about your use. - Gum comes in 2 and 4 mg. For cigarette smokers, the usual guide is 4 mg if they smoke within 30 minutes of waking, otherwise 2 mg, but that isn't a validated vape-dose conversion. Given that cold turkey left you struggling, ask the pharmacist about a patch plus gum, rather than assuming you have to choose one. Bring the vape packaging or a photo of its label, and tell them how soon after waking you vape and whether you use it overnight. Nicotine replacement isn't specifically FDA-approved for quitting vaping, but clinicians use it to help manage withdrawal.
