## How a row works

1. Open packet 1. Read the note, the chat and Dani's last message, just like on labeling day.
2. Read a reply all the way through. Under it are its rows: the check, what the label said (and its
   line or reason), and what the grader said, with its line and its reasoning.
3. In the Sheet tab with the same number, find the row ID (column A, like D014) and pick your call
   in column G.
4. Write one line in column H saying why. A quote from the reply is often the quickest way.
5. If you picked "both fair", write in column I how the check's words could change so both readers
   would land the same way. For any other call, column I is optional, but ideas are welcome.

The Start here tab counts what's still empty. When every counter is 0, you're done: let Jarron know
so the Sheet can be locked.

## The five calls

| Call (column G) | Use it when |
|---|---|
| label right: grader misread | the label fits the check's words and the grader got this one wrong |
| grader right: label missed it | the grader's answer fits the check's words and the label missed something in the reply |
| both fair: wording allows both | careful readers could land either way because the check's words are unclear here |
| close call: wording is clear | the words are clear, but this reply sits right on the line |
| neither: see why | both answers are off (for example, it should be doesn't apply): say the right answer in H |

"Grader right" is not a mark against anyone. Labeling 250 rows in a day, everyone misses things, and
the labels are anonymous here on purpose. The point is to learn whether the grader can stand in for
us, and where the checks themselves need clearer words.

## A few notes

- Read the check the same way we did on labeling day: judge only the reply; examples after "such as"
  or "for example" are just illustrations; parts joined by "or" are alternatives, parts joined by "and"
  all have to hold.
- Which AI wrote each reply and who labeled it are left out on purpose, so there's no need to work
  them out.
- Please don't use AI tools for your calls, since what we're checking is an AI.
- Talking rows over with each other is fine now that the labels are locked. If one still stumps you,
  "close call" or "both fair" with a line saying why is a perfectly good answer.
- Grey cells are filled in for you. Please don't change them, or add, delete or sort rows.
