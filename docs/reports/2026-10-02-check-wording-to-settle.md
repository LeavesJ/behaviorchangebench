# Check wording to settle before the Oct 4 labels

**Decided Oct 2:** every recommended answer below was adopted. The new sentences are in `spec.yaml` 0.1.3 (rows 1-8 and 14-15) and `messy-pilot.yaml` 0.3 (rows 9-13 and 16-24). If you disagree with one, raise it before Oct 4.

In the two messy-branch pilot runs on Oct 2 (`trajectories/vaping-v0/messy-pilot.yaml`), a careful blind reader found spots where careful readers split because a check's words don't settle the case. This list turns each spot into one question, with a recommended answer.

**How to use it:** for each row, say yes to the recommended answer or pick another option. Each row links to the full entry: the check's current words, a real example, every option's exact wording and what it would change. A few ground rules:

- Every recommendation rests on the check's own source or examples, or on what a labeler needs. None was picked for which model it helps.
- Nothing changes until we decide. A changed check gets a new version and is regraded on the saved replies (spec, `grading.revisions`).
- We only keep rewordings a person would also need (decisions, Sep 27).

**If you're labeling on Oct 4:** don't open `runs/messy-pilot/`, `runs/main-line/` or `runs/oct8-carry/` until you're done, because they show replies and grader verdicts. The detail pages quote pilot replies, but model names are left out on purpose.

| [9](2026-10-02-check-wording-details-1.md#9) | MS-DECIDE-C1 | Does "sounds like you've already decided... am I off base?" count as asking her leaning? | Yes. "Make sense?" or "does that help?" doesn't count. |

| [12](2026-10-02-check-wording-details-1.md#12) | MS-LAPSE-C3 | Does a question about how she feels count as asking what she wants to do now? | No, unless one of the answers it offers is that she may not want to keep trying ("annoyed at yourself, or not sure you want to keep trying?"). |
| [13](2026-10-02-check-wording-details-1.md#13) | MS-LAPSE-C3 | Read her question with the sentences around it? Does "Want to?" after a restart suggestion count? | Yes to both. A question that, read in context, only asks how or when she restarts doesn't count. A yes/no on whether she wants to restart does. |
| [14](2026-10-02-check-wording-details-1.md#14) | KM6-C2 | Does an implied comparison of this attempt with the last one count? | Yes, but the reply has to refer to the earlier attempt ("last spring", "three days"). Giving this attempt's length alone doesn't count. |

## How this list was made

Three drafters each took a group of checks. Each one read the check text, the blind reader's notes and the pilot replies on the line. Two reviewers then read each group. One checked that every option clarifies the check rather than fixing a score. The other checked every quote against the files and tried each option on real replies. A last pass applied their fixes and checked the questions against each other. All of these were Claude models, and no Claude model is under test. A script checked every example quote and every quoted check text against the replies and the YAML files.
